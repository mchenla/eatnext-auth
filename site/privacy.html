EatNext Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026. Operator: Min Chen. Contact: desmymail@gmail.com.

Information and purposes

EatNext processes your account email and authentication information to sign you in. When you use connected tools, it stores the food catalog, nutrition labels and estimates, calibration notes, meals, activity, body-weight records, inventory, goals and source notes that you provide. These records may contain health-related information. They support food logging, target adjustment and nutrition coaching. Email sent to support is used to handle your request.

Storage and services

Supabase provides account authentication and the private database. The deployed database region is US East. Account access is restricted through authentication and per-user database policies. Requests and selected records returned by tools are processed by the ChatGPT or Codex host you use, under that host’s own policies and settings. GitHub Pages hosts these public pages; infrastructure providers may process network and operational logs under their own policies. Do not submit information you do not want processed by these services.

No advertising or sale

Min Chen does not sell EatNext user data or use it for advertising. Data is processed to operate the service and respond to support and privacy requests.

Retention and requests

Account records are retained until you request deletion. Email the address above to request access, correction, export or account-data deletion. Account ownership may need verification. Deletion from EatNext does not delete conversations held by your host or records held by infrastructure providers under their separate retention policies. Do not send passwords or tokens.

Age and changes

EatNext is for users aged 18 and older. Policy updates will be published here with an updated effective date.
