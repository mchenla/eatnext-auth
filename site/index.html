EatNext

Adaptive nutrition coaching for fat loss and muscle gain.

Created by Min Chen. EatNext is free, with no purchases or subscriptions.

What it does

Choose a goal mode, adjust daily calorie and macro targets to reported activity, log meals, and plan what to eat next. Connected tools store your food catalog, meal records, activity, body-weight records, inventory and targets in your account.

Getting connected

Database features require an EatNext account and sign-in authorization. Account access is currently limited; public onboarding and marketplace review are being prepared.
