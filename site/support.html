EatNext Support

EatNext is developed by Min Chen and is free, with no purchases or subscriptions.

Contact

For support, privacy questions, or account-data deletion requests, email desmymail@gmail.com.

Describe the issue and the account email if needed. Do not send passwords, sign-in links, tokens, or unnecessary health information. Do not post private meal or health records in public GitHub issues. Account ownership may need to be verified before a data request can be fulfilled.

Connection help

Database features require an authorized EatNext account. Public onboarding and marketplace review are being prepared; a failed connection does not mean a meal or target was saved.
